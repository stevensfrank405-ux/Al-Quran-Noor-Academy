NOORANI QAIDA

Noorani
Qaida.
Begin with the basics.

Every confident reader starts with a first letter. Explore the sounds, shapes, and simple patterns that form the foundation of Quran reading.

✧

A CLEARER PICTURE

What you’ll explore.

01

Meet the Arabic letters

Recognise individual letters and how their shapes change at the beginning, middle, and end of a word.

02

Explore short vowels

Practise fatha, kasra, and damma, connecting each sign with its sound.

03

Build connected reading

Work through joined letters, sukoon, shaddah, and tanween with careful repetition.

04

Take the next step

Bring the foundations together in short reading exercises before discussing a move to Quran recitation.

BETWEEN LESSONS

A little practice goes a long way.

Spend a few calm minutes recognising letters and reading the lesson aloud. Practise accuracy before speed, and bring unfamiliar sounds to your next lesson.

This is an introductory course outline. Lesson content, pace, materials, and progression should be agreed with the academy based on the learner’s needs. No fixed completion time is promised.

Your course at a glance

Who it’s for

Complete beginners, children learning Arabic letters, and adults who want to revisit the basics.

Learning direction

Recognise Arabic letters and vowel signs, join sounds carefully, and develop a foundation for reading short Quranic passages.

Format & scheduling

Online learning for local and international students. Confirm teacher availability, lesson format, time zone, and fees before enrolling.
