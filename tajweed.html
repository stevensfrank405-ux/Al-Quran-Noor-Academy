QURAN WITH TAJWEED

Quran
with Tajweed.
Grow with intention.

Read with greater awareness, one sound at a time. Explore the principles that help bring clarity and care to your Quran recitation.

✧

A CLEARER PICTURE

What you’ll explore.

01

Understand your starting point

Review a short passage and identify which reading foundations and pronunciation habits need attention.

02

Give each letter its sound

Practise articulation points (makharij) and the characteristics that distinguish similar letters.

03

Recognise recitation patterns

Explore introductory rules for noon sakinah, tanween, meem sakinah, and appropriate elongation (madd).

04

Apply and revise

Read short passages, revisit corrections, and discuss appropriate places to pause and begin.

BETWEEN LESSONS

A little practice goes a long way.

Revisit a small passage slowly. Focus on one correction at a time, and note any word or rule you want to review with your teacher.

This is an introductory course outline. Lesson content, pace, materials, and progression should be agreed with the academy based on the learner’s needs. No fixed completion time is promised.

۞

Your course at a glance

Who it’s for

Learners who can recognise Arabic letters and read connected words, including students returning to Quran reading after a break.

Learning direction

Strengthen pronunciation, recognise common Tajweed rules, and apply them gradually while reading.

Format & scheduling

Online learning for local and international students. Confirm teacher availability, lesson format, time zone, and fees before enrolling.
