PRIVACY

Your information.
Handled with clarity.

How this website works, what the inquiry form does, and the services your browser may connect to.

✧

The inquiry form

The inquiry form uses the details you enter to prepare a message inside your browser. The site does not submit those details to the academy, store them in a database, or save them in browser storage. Copying the message places it on your device’s clipboard. Downloading creates a text file on your device.

Sharing your message

If you later send that message using email, WhatsApp, or another service, that service’s privacy practices apply. Share only what is needed to arrange lessons. Do not include financial details, identification documents, or sensitive personal information in the inquiry.

Children’s information

A parent or guardian should handle inquiries for children. Use the adult’s name and contact details. There is no need to include a child’s full name, date of birth, school, or home address in the initial inquiry.

Hosting and fonts

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Your browser may request fonts from Google Fonts, which can receive technical request information such as your IP address. If those fonts are unavailable, the website uses fallback fonts.

Cookies and analytics

The application does not set cookies or include analytics scripts. Hosting and external service providers may process technical information according to their own policies.

Future updates

If a direct contact channel, booking service, or form backend is added, this page should be updated to explain how information is sent and handled before that service is used.
