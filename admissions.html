A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Questions?
You’re welcome here.

Can I learn from outside Pakistan?

Yes, the online learning format is intended for local and international students. Include your country and time zone in your inquiry so suitable lesson times can be discussed.

Is this suitable for complete beginners?

Noorani Qaida is the starting point for learners new to Arabic letters and Quran reading. Share your experience when you enquire so the right starting level can be discussed.

Can adults join as well as children?

The courses are intended for children and adults. A parent or guardian should handle inquiries and arrangements for a child.

What do I need for online lessons?

A reliable internet connection, a phone, tablet, or computer with audio, and a quiet place to learn. The lesson platform and materials will be confirmed when arranging your classes.

What are the fees and lesson timings?

Fees, teacher availability, trial arrangements, and the lesson schedule should be confirmed directly with the academy before enrolling. No payment is collected on this website.
